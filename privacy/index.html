PRIVACY POLICY

Your media and accounts stay under your control.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

What Orynthian Post processes

Orynthian Post works with creator-owned media selected from folders the creator configures. It may analyze videos, create locally stored derivatives, and generate captions or other metadata for creator review. Original source media is preserved by the application workflow.

TikTok connection data

When a creator connects TikTok, Orynthian Post uses TikTok OAuth to obtain the permissions the creator authorizes. It uses connected account identity information to show and map the account in the desktop app. Access and refresh tokens are stored using Windows secure storage and are not exposed to the app's renderer or AI exchange.

Publishing actions

Orynthian Post can prepare uploads or posts to supported third-party platforms only after creator review and explicit approval of the exact media, copy, destination, mode, and applicable privacy setting. It does not claim unattended automatic publishing. TikTok receives the content and metadata needed for an approved TikTok action.

Disconnecting and deletion

Use the TikTok DISCONNECT control in Orynthian Post to remove the connected account record and encrypted TikTok tokens from the app's local state. TikTok authorization may also be revoked through TikTok's own account controls. Locally created derivatives, drafts, and other app data remain on the creator's device unless removed there.

Retention and third parties

Orynthian Post stores local workflow state and selected media-related artifacts as needed to provide the desktop workflow. Connected social platforms process data under their own policies. Orynthian Post does not sell TikTok user data.
